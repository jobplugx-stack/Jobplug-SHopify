 Description  
Career Transition Program  
Designed for professionals pivoting into tech, engineering, or high-paying industries  
Make your next career move with clarity and confidence. Our Career Transition Program is built specifically for ambitious professionals from non-traditional or unrelated backgrounds who are ready to break into high-demand industries.  
What’s Included:   
Tailored Resume & LinkedIn Revamp 
Transform your resume and online presence to align with your target role. We highlight your transferable skills and position you as a competitive candidate in your new industry.   
Custom Career Roadmap & Learning Resources 
Receive a personalized strategy that outlines the steps, certifications, and knowledge needed to make your transition successful—plus curated resources to help you learn as you go.   
Mock Interviews for Non-Traditional Backgrounds 
Practice interviews focused on helping you confidently tell your story, address career gaps or transitions, and showcase your value in a new field.   
Industry Networking & Strategy Call 
Get insider strategies and warm intros where possible. Learn how to navigate networking in your chosen field and make connections that accelerate your path forward.    
Best For:  
Career changers, professionals transitioning into tech, engineering, or other high-paying and high-growth industries.
