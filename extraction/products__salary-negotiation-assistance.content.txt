What’s Included in the Service
1. Compensation Benchmarking
Thorough market research to identify your true earning potential based on role, location, and industry.
You’ll receive a custom report comparing your current or target role across top companies.
2. Offer Review & Negotiation Strategy
Detailed analysis and optimization of your job offer.
Includes tailored negotiation scripts, email templates, and proven strategies to help you maximize leverage.
3. Salary Negotiation Coaching
One-on-one coaching sessions to prepare you for real conversations.
We’ll focus on tone, timing, and key messaging to help you negotiate with confidence.
4. Total Compensation Maximization
We go beyond base salary—identifying opportunities for increased equity, bonuses, benefits, relocation support, and more.
Includes review of PTO, insurance, 401(k) matching, and flexibility perks.
5. Promotion & Raise Positioning
For those seeking growth within their current company, we help you make the case for a raise or promotion.
Includes performance framing, data-driven support, and internal communication strategy.
6. Personalized Salary Roadmap
A step-by-step plan built around your goals, role, and timeline.
You’ll walk away with a clear path to higher pay and stronger benefits.
