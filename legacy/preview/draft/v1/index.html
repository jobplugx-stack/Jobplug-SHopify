07 Questions — good questions, straight answers

Anything on your mind?

Who is JobPlug for?

Recent graduates, professionals seeking a better role, and people changing careers — across industries, with specialized services for technical roles.

Do I need to know which service I want?

No. Book a consultation or complete the intake form to share your goals, and we’ll help you decide which service fits.

Can you apply to jobs on my behalf?

Yes. AutoApply+ and the Job Lock-In Package include application support. Confirm the service period and scope before purchasing.

What does the 30-day commitment include?

A free career strategy session if no interview is secured within 30 days. It does not guarantee a job offer or a refund. Confirm eligibility and the start date during your consultation.

What happens after I purchase?

Onboarding starts with an email asking for your profile details and résumé. Complete your intake and follow the instructions so support can be tailored to your goals.
