## 1. Services and current descriptions

**Company one-liner** (`Presentations/JobPlug_Business_Overview_1.pptx`, slide 2): "We do the job search for you. JobPlug sources candidates, builds profiles, applies to roles daily, and delivers interview-ready candidates." Pillars: Built from Experience ("Founded after firsthand frustration with the job search process"), Profile-First Approach, We Apply Daily ("Dozens of matching applications submitted every business day"), Interview Focused ("Success is measured in interviews landed, not applications sent").

**Done-for-you descriptor** (`Emails and Templates/JobPlug_Interview_Update_Existing_Customers_Email.html`): "A done for you career placement company. We find the jobs. We submit the applications. We secure the interviews. You show up and win."

**What JobPlug actually does** (`Presentations/JobPlug_Sales_Pitch_Closer.pptx` slide 3, identical in `Archive/Job Plug Essentials/JobPlug Sales Pitch Closer 2026.pdf`): "We become their job search team for 30 days - any industry, any level."
- We build the resume: "Action verb + impact + result, with real metrics from their actual work history."
- We source the roles: "Direct-employer roles matched to their target titles - never staffing agencies or recruiter junk."
- We apply for them: "Full applications, every day, for 30 days straight. Not a handful. Not Easy Apply."
- We manage the inbox: "Every recruiter reply, every interview request - tracked and handled so nothing slips through."
- We give them a portal: "Live visibility into every application, every resume version, every status update."

**Differentiators** (same deck slide 4; `Archive/Job Plug Essentials/jp behind the scenes brief pdf.pdf`): real applications not mass-blast automation; direct-employer roles only, no staffing agencies; dedicated team working the search daily; resumes built on real metrics; live portal; "An honest process - no fabricated numbers, ever"; tagline "We do the work. You see everything." / "No black box. No guesswork."

**Service components in the signed-style agreement** (`Contracts/JobPlug_Terms_and_Conditions_2.docx`, identical text in `Archive/Job Plug Essentials/JobPlug Terms and Conditions 2026.docx`): profile intake link; custom resume build and optimization (ATS); personal job board; daily applications submitted directly to company career pages; full employer communication management with real-time notifications; guaranteed interview; one complimentary mock interview.

**Resume method**: "JobPlug Bullet Formula" = Action Verb + Impact Statement + Result/Metrics; ATS keywords in skills; one-page; built with Claude then Beam Jobs (`Presentations/JobPlug_Business_Overview_1.pptx` slide 6). One master resume, extra versions only if hits are low (`Docs/JobPlug_CEO_Process_Flow.pdf`).

**Add-on: Interview Master Package ($200)** (`Contracts/JobPlug_Terms_and_Conditions_2.docx`): "a full walkthrough of the entire interview process, built specifically around you, your target role, and the company you are going for." Company research in full detail; prep for "the level above the role"; three rounds: Round 1 Foundation (most intensive, find weak points), Round 2 Refinement, Round 3 Final Seal (full simulation). Must be requested and scheduled in advance.

**Alternate name for prep add-on**: "Interview Expert Package" - "A professional in your field runs a full recorded mock interview with you" (`Emails and Templates/jobplug_post_consultation_welcome_8.html`). No price given.

**Included interview prep after an interview lands** (same email): 3-stage prep call: Your Story, Project Review, Final Run-Through, usually a few days before the interview.

**Career change add-on**: "Want a career change after your consultation? That's outside our original scope so an additional fee applies." No price (`jobplug_post_consultation_welcome_8.html`).

**Not offered / out of scope per current docs**: LinkedIn optimization, cover letters, salary negotiation, coaching are in the generic mockup `Emails and Templates/jobplug_redesign.html` and in CTC docs, but NOT in any JobPlug agreement. Treat as unverified.

**Quality rules** (`_Claude Project Pack/Business core/JobPlug_Position_Details_v2.docx`, `Team/JobPlug_Team_Plan_Oct5-9.pdf`): no LinkedIn Easy Apply; full applications only; no staffing/temp roles; do not apply if client does not meet requirements.

## 8. FAQs and common customer questions (from sales guide, emails, process docs)

- **How do I know this works?** Portal shows every application in real time from day one (`Presentations/JobPlug_Sales_Pitch_Closer.pptx`).
- **What if I don't get a job in 30 days?** Contract: guaranteed at least one interview in 30 business days; no promise of a job offer (sections 3-4). Sales deck says "not a guarantee" (conflict).
- **I can't afford $300-400.** Payment plan: $100 down, rest within 30 days (Package 2) or due on offer (Package 3, $500).
- **Can't I do this myself?** Yes; cost is the 2-3 hours a day it takes to do well.
- **What if I'm in a niche field?** Industry-agnostic, same process.
- **What does "30 days" mean?** 30 business days, Mon-Fri, excluding federal holidays; start-date rules in section 3.
- **What do I get access to?** Portal login within ~48 hours of intake; dedicated job-search Gmail; Starred folder.
- **Where do recruiter emails go?** The job-search Gmail; JobPlug stars and notifies. Skip the main inbox (rejections live there).
- **Why is my own phone number on my resume?** Since Sept 25 2026 recruiters call clients directly; JobPlug moved away from relaying calls to apply for more jobs (`Emails and Templates/JobPlug_Interview_Update_Existing_Customers_Email.html`).
- **What do I do when a recruiter calls?** Unknown number: voicemail; known name: answer; book the interview, don't interview on the spot; get role/location; text JobPlug within 24 hours; 10 call/email scripts in that email.
- **Can I mention JobPlug?** Yes.
- **Interview prep?** One complimentary mock (agreement) plus 3-stage prep call; Interview Master $200 for full 3 rounds.
- **Will I be told about offers?** Yes, but Package 2/3 details held until balance is paid (clause 5).
- **Weekly reports?** Every week by email; sample format in welcome email.
- **Do you apply via LinkedIn Easy Apply?** No.
- **Referral?** $50 per paying referral, uncapped.
- **Career change?** Extra fee.
- **Is a refund available?** Deposits non-refundable once service starts (agreement). Contradictory CTC-era wording promises refund.
