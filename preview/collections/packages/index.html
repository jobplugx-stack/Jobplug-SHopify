Who is JobPlug for?

JobPlug supports recent graduates, professionals seeking a better role, and people changing careers. The business works across industries, with specialized services for technical roles.

Do I need to know which service I want?

No. Book a consultation or complete the intake form to share your goals. We can help you decide which service fits your situation.

Can you apply to jobs on my behalf?

Yes. AutoApply+ and the Job Lock-In Package include application support. AutoApply+ lists targeted applications and weekly tracking reports. Confirm the service period and scope before purchasing.

What does the 30-day commitment include?

The Job Lock-In Package offers a free career strategy session if no interview is secured within 30 days. It does not guarantee a job offer or a refund. Confirm eligibility and the start date during your consultation.

Where does my intake information go?

The intake form is JobPlug’s existing Google Form, embedded here for convenience. When you submit, Google Forms saves the response directly to that form. Google displays the submission confirmation.

What happens after I purchase a service?

The current onboarding process starts with an email asking for your profile details and résumé. Complete your intake and follow the instructions provided by JobPlug so support can be tailored to your goals.
