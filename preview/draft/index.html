08 Questions

Straight answers.

When do the 30 business days start?

[Confirm start rule.] Business days are Monday to Friday. Your window is counted from your start date, once your intake and payment are complete. See the Terms for the full definition.

What about holidays?

Holidays don’t count. We also skip the business day before and after each holiday. For example, if Labor Day falls on a Monday, we exclude that Monday, the Friday before and the Tuesday after, so you get the best possible shot at landing interviews.

What counts as an interview?

[Confirm definition.] A scheduled phone, video or in-person conversation with an employer or recruiter about a role we applied to for you.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, if we don’t secure at least one interview within your 30 business days and you’ve met your responsibilities. That’s the one exception. See the Terms for how to request it.

How does “Start today” work?

You pay $99 today to begin right away, then the $300 balance within your 30 business days. We send weekly reminders. If the balance isn’t paid, services pause and your place can be cancelled with no refund.

Can I transfer my package or refer a friend?

Packages are not transferable and expire after 30 business days. If you’d like to refer someone, request a refund first and, once we accept it, they can buy their own package.
