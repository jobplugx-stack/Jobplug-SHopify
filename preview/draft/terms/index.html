4Payment before work begins

You pay first. After you pay, you complete our intake form, and only then does your information enter our system and your work begin. We do not collect your information for service, or begin any work on your behalf, until your Enrollment Payment has been received and cleared.

Under the Start Today plan you must keep a valid payment card on file, and you authorize us to charge the $300 Balance to it during your Service Window. We send reminders before the charge. Payment must be made through JobPlug’s official checkout or another method we approve in writing.

5Your 30 business days

Your Service Window is counted in Business Days only. Weekends are not counted. Holidays are not counted, and we also exclude the Business Day immediately before and after each holiday (the “Holiday Buffer Days”) so that your applications reach employers at the best times. For example, when a holiday falls on a Monday, the preceding Friday, that Monday and the following Tuesday are not counted.

Start Date. If you enroll on or before the 15th of the month, your 30 Business Days start once your onboarding is complete. If you enroll after the 15th, your official 30 Business Days start on the 1st of the following month. In either case we begin applying and engaging with you right away, as a head start at no additional cost, so that you are set up for the best possible results.

Your package expires at the end of your Service Window. At that time we may invite you to purchase another package.
